How to read this page

Every sector below follows the same structure: the case, the openings, what it needs and the licensing route.

Where a constraint is real, it is stated. A sector with abundant resource and no processing capacity is an opportunity and a warning at the same time, and pretending otherwise wastes everybody’s first meeting.

Figures throughout are indicative and provided for shortlisting. Project-level data — financial models, land documentation and offtake detail — is released to registered investors under NDA.
