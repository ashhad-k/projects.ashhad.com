(01)

Do I need an agent or a lawyer to register a company?

+

No. Every service is designed to be completed directly by the applicant, and the portal walks through each step. Many companies still use an agent for convenience or for translation, but nothing on this platform requires one, and using one does not make an application move faster.

(02)

How long does it take to get a trade licence?

+

Five to seven working days from a complete file. Trade name reservation is same-day, and licence renewals and amendments are faster still. The published decision time for every service appears next to it, and the portal shows which desk holds your file at any moment.

(03)

Which legal structure should I choose?

+

Most operating businesses register a limited liability company: liability is limited to the company, ownership can be split, and it can be wholly foreign-owned. A sole proprietorship is simpler but offers no separation between you and the business. A joint stock company suits a wider shareholder base, and a branch registers the foreign company itself rather than creating a new one.

(04)

Can I register more than one business activity?

+

Yes, and you should register everything you intend to do. Activities determine your licence category, the inspecting authority and any sector approvals. Operating outside your registered activities is the most common compliance failure, and adding an activity later is an amendment rather than a new registration.

(05)

Do I have to visit an office?

+

No. Applications, payments, document uploads and tracking are all online. Physical inspection applies only to specific industrial activities, and where it does, it is scheduled through the portal.

(06)

What if my documents are in a language other than Dari or Pashto?

+

Foreign-language documents must be translated by a certified translator and, where they originate abroad, legalised in the country of issue first — the translation has to capture the legalisation stamps. English-language documents are accepted for review, but the final record is kept in the official language.

(07)

Do you keep my original documents?

+

No original is retained. Everything is uploaded as a scan, and originals are produced only if a file is later challenged. Nothing has to be posted or delivered by hand.

(08)

My registry extract is four months old. Is that a problem?

+

Yes — extracts must be dated within the last three months at the point of filing. This is the most common reason a foreign file is returned, because the extract often ages out while the rest of the documents are being legalised.

(09)

Can I upload documents in stages?

+

Yes. An application is saved as you go and can be left incomplete. The clock on the published decision time starts when the file is complete and submitted, not when you begin.

(10)

Can a foreign investor own 100% of an Afghan company?

+

Yes, across the priority sectors, with no requirement to take a local partner. Five sectors — mining, energy, banking, telecoms and pharmaceuticals — require an additional regulator approval, but that is a licence to operate in the sector rather than a limit on who may own the company.

(11)

Is there a minimum capital requirement?

+

There is no general minimum for a limited liability company. Capital is declared rather than deposited. Sector regulators set their own thresholds, and industrial land allocation is assessed against the scale of the declared investment.

(12)

How do I get profits out of the country?

+

Through the banking system, in the currency the capital came in, once the period’s tax obligations are settled. The investment registration and the inbound transfer records are what the bank asks for, which is why registering the investment at the outset matters more than it appears to at the time.

(13)

Can a foreign-owned company buy land?

+

Freehold title is reserved for Afghan nationals and Afghan-registered companies. An Afghan-registered company that is wholly foreign-owned takes industrial land on a long lease — typically thirty years, renewable — allocated through the industrial park authority with utilities identified in advance.

(14)

Do I need to be resident in Afghanistan?

+

No. You need a named local representative resident in Afghanistan to receive official notices. That is an administrative appointment: it carries no ownership stake and no authority over the business unless you grant it separately.

(15)

What does registration actually cost?

+

A new trade licence is AFN 8,000, trade name reservation AFN 1,000, renewal AFN 5,500 and an amendment AFN 4,500. Branch registration is AFN 15,000 and trademark registration AFN 12,000. Verification is free. The full schedule is published, and the exact amount is confirmed in the portal before you pay.

(16)

Are there charges beyond the published fees?

+

No. The published fee is the fee. Nothing is charged if a file cannot be accepted, and if you believe a fee has been assessed incorrectly against the schedule, there is a form for that and no charge for using it.

(17)

How do I pay?

+

In the portal, by bank transfer or card, against a reference tied to your application. A receipt is issued immediately and is retrievable from your account at any time afterwards.

(18)

What happens if my licence expires?

+

Renewal opens sixty days before expiry. Renewing on time keeps your licence number and avoids the lapse fee. Letting a licence expire means re-registering from the start, which is slower and more expensive than renewing.

(19)

I am changing my address and my activities. One filing or two?

+

One. An amendment can carry several changes at once — address, activities, capital, ownership — for a single AFN 4,500 fee and a two-to-four working day decision. Filing them separately costs more and takes longer.

(20)

Can I check a company before I trade with it?

+

Yes, free and without an account, on the Verify & Search page. Enter the licence number or the company name and you get the live register record — status, activities, province and validity dates. Licences issued since the portal went live also carry a QR code that resolves to the same record.

(21)

How do I challenge a decision?

+

File a complaint or appeal through the portal at no charge. It receives a tracked reference and a response within ten to fifteen working days. Because every application, payment and decision is timestamped, the record of what happened is not in dispute.

(22)

Who do I contact about a specific sector approval?

+

The portal routes sector approvals to the relevant regulator and tracks them on the same file, so you do not approach each authority separately. For questions before you apply, the Investment Facilitation Unit will connect you to the right desk.

(23)

What languages is support available in?

+

English, Dari and Pashto, by email, phone and through the portal. The site itself is published in English first, with Dari and Pashto to follow.

(24)

Something on this site looks wrong. Where do I report it?

+

Through the contact page, or by replying to any notification from the portal. Corrections to published fees, decision times or legislative references are treated as priority items.
