Now Available: Business Passport applications through the E-Afghans Portal

We are pleased to announce that Business Passport applications are now available through the E-Afghans Portal, providing Afghan business owners with a convenient and secure way to apply online from anywhere in the world.

Why Apply for a Business Passport?

A Business Passport is designed for Afghan entrepreneurs and business owners who require a passport that supports their business and international travel needs.

Through the E-Afghans Portal, the entire application process can be completed online, eliminating unnecessary visits and simplifying the experience.

Applying for your Business Passport is easy:

✓ Sign in to your E-Afghans account or create a new account
✓ Access the Business Passport service
✓ Complete the online application form
✓ Upload your required documents
✓ Make your payment securely online
✓ Track your application status in real time
✓ Receive updates throughout the processing journey
✓ Have your approved passport delivered to your registered address

Who Can Apply?

You may be eligible if you:

• Are an Afghan citizen
• Have an existing Afghan passport
• Own a business
• Hold an active business license
• Meet the application requirements and eligibility criteria

Documents Required

To complete your application, prepare the following:

• Afghan Tazkira (National ID)
• Existing Afghan Passport
• Passport-sized Photograph
• Residence ID (if applicable)
• Active Business License
