Your record Your record starts with one document, and fills itself

What was lifted off the passport is yours to check and correct. Nothing else is assumed — the post does not reach into anybody else’s system to find you. From here the record fills as work is done: every document this consulate issues lands in it, sealed, and is never asked for twice.
