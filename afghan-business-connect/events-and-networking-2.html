Afghan Business Excellence Awards 2025

May 10, 2025
6:30 PM – 10:30 PM
Invite-Only Event
Afghan Business Connect

Overview

The Afghan Business Excellence Awards 2025 is the premier recognition platform celebrating the achievements, innovation, and impact of Afghan-led businesses and professionals in the UAE.

Now in its inaugural edition, this red-carpet gala aims to bring together business leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, and changemakers from across the Afghan community to honor outstanding contributions across key sectors.

Whether you're a startup founder, SME owner, corporate leader, or community influencer, this night is dedicated to acknowledging your excellence.

Award Categories Include:

Business of the Year 2025
Young Entrepreneur Award
Women in Leadership Award
Most Innovative Startup
Excellence in Community Impact
Lifetime Achievement Award
Tech Disruptor of the Year
Best Afghan-owned SME in UAE
