About Green Harvest Agriculture

Green Harvest Agriculture is revolutionizing sustainable farming in Afghanistan and the UAE by providing eco-friendly, AI-powered smart irrigation systems and direct-to-market digital solutions for farmers. Our mission is to improve crop efficiency, reduce water waste by 40%, and empower farmers with affordable technology to scale production and increase earnings.

Problem We Solve:

  • 70% of Afghan farmers still rely on outdated irrigation methods, leading to poor yields and high water consumption.
  • Limited access to marketplaces forces farmers to sell products below value.

Our Solution:

  • AI-powered precision irrigation that saves 40% water usage.
  • A digital marketplace connecting farmers directly to UAE buyers & food suppliers.
  • Training & mentorship programs for sustainable organic farming.
